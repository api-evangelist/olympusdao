A New Standard for Borrowing Today, most lending systems are structured to favor lenders over borrowers. Borrowers often face opaque risk parameters, liquidation cascades triggered by oracles, and dependency on third-party liquidity. Whether it’s through variable interest rates or sudden liquidations, the borrower remains structurally disadvantaged.
